# Grain-size variable map

- `D_bl`: radiation-pressure blowout diameter at total beta=0.5; derived, not observed.
- `D_min`: lower cutoff of the collisional size distribution; may be physical-model derived.
- `D_char`: characteristic emitting size; not constrained by the project SED fit.
- `blowout_diameter_um=20.0`: fixed hard-coded argument in `run_joint_map_visibility_posterior.py`; no posterior or source citation.
- Lawler 2014 `15 +/- 8 um`: preferred physical-grain-model minimum diameter, not a measured blowout diameter.
