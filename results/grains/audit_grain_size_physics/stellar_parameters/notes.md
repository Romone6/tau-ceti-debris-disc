# Adopted stellar inputs

The analytic radiation-pressure calculation requires L and M, not Teff or radius. The project adopts L=0.52 +/- 0.03 L_sun and M=0.783 +/- 0.012 M_sun. Teff/radius were not part of the current posterior and are therefore not silently inserted. Q_pr is treated as an explicit grain-composition scenario parameter.

Scenario-draw medians: D_bl(rad)=0.950 um; D_bl(total, 0.1 solar wind)=0.950 um.
