The 20 um diameter used in the diagnostic collisional calculation is a fixed, undocumented parameter substitution rather than a derived Tau Ceti radiation-pressure blowout size. For a diameter convention, beta=3LQ_pr/(8 pi cGM rho D), so the beta=0.5 blowout diameter is D_bl=3LQ_pr/(4 pi cGM rho). Using the adopted L=0.52 L_sun and M=0.783 M_sun gives sub-micron-to-few-micron radiation-only diameters across compact-grain scenarios; a Tau-specific wind bound below 0.1 solar does not raise the result to 20 um. The Lawler 15 +/- 8 um value is retained as a physical-model minimum-grain scenario, not as D_bl. Because the implemented q=11/6 Eq. 14 scales f_max as D_cut^-1/2, using a larger D_min increases f/f_max, but this is a lower-cutoff sensitivity and must not be presented as a physical blowout inference.
